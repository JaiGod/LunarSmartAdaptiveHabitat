Scenario simulator (biomedical simulation)

Injects a condition into the simulated wearable stream so you can demo how the AI detects, triages and responds. Mild = silent adjustment · Moderate = options offered to the crew member · Severe = escalation to the Chief Medical Officer.

Circadian rhythm (crew-wide)

Simplified alertness model: A(t) = cos(2π(t − 15) / 24), t in hours. Peak mid-afternoon, lowest around 03:00. The dot is the current habitat time.

How the waves are built

  • Pulse: y = sin(2πft) + 0.35·sin(4πft + 0.9), with f = heart rate ÷ 60 Hz. The trace turns red when heart rate is well above that person's baseline.
  • Respiration: y = cos(2πft), with f = breaths per minute ÷ 60 Hz. Breathing rate rises with the stress signal.
  • Inject a condition on the Crew Health tab and watch the waves tighten as the frequency climbs.

Fault simulator

High CO₂ feeds into the crew model: air quality is a sensor for cognitive fatigue and irritability.

Hard limits (what the AI never does)

  • No cameras or microphones inside a private pod. In pod privacy mode psychological monitoring pauses. Only life-safety vitals (heart, oxygen, CO₂) stay on.
  • If a crew member declines an intervention, the AI stops and logs it. It does not retry or work around the refusal.
  • Never administers medication or changes a care plan. Severe cases go to a human (CMO, or Commander + ground if the CMO is the one affected).
  • In shared zones, adjustments are shown without naming who triggered them.
  • Any crew member can override the scene in any zone. Manual choice beats the AI.
