LunaEar
Hear beyond the horizon.

A small earbud that speaks only when it matters. LunaEar gives morning summaries, guided breathing and EVA mode prompts, and during an emergency it tells you which way to go, with spatial audio placing the voice in the direction of the shelter.

Features

What LunaEar does

01

Morning brief

Sleep quality, today's first task and anything that changed overnight, in under 15 seconds.

02

Guided breathing

A 60-second exercise offered after a high-strain period, never forced.

03

EVA mode

Route prompts, suit oxygen call-outs and crew check-ins with no screen to look at.

04

Directional alerts

In an emergency the voice comes from the direction you need to go.

Privacy rule for every LUNA-X device: raw data stays on the device, analysis runs on the device, and wellness data is shared only with the crew member's consent. Nothing here diagnoses; a human always decides.
