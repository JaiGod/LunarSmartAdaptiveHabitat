Looking After the People Who Explore the Moon

Months from home, an astronaut's hardest days aren't always the dangerous ones. Sometimes they're the long, quiet, exhausting ones. LUNA-X is a family of wearables (a helmet, a glove and a boot) that carries the habitat's LUNA wellness AI out onto the crew, so someone is always watching for fatigue, stress and danger, even when no one says a word.

A day on the Moon

One Crew Member, One Shift

07:00 · Wake upThe helmet powers on beside the bunk, checks its sensors and links to the habitat. The crew starts the day already knowing the air, the power and the weather are fine.
10:30 · GreenhouseThe glove scans a struggling plant and flags low moisture before the leaves turn. A small fix now saves a week of food later.
14:00 · EVAOn uneven regolith, the boot feels a stride getting shorter and a stance getting wider. It stiffens the ankle and suggests a rest stop before a slip can happen.
18:30 · Back insideLUNA notices quieter conversations and slower reactions. Instead of an alarm, an orange glow suggests time in the greenhouse, and the habitat lights warm up to match.

"That's one small step for man, one giant leap for mankind." Neil Armstrong, Apollo 11, 1969. Every step since depends on keeping the people taking them safe and well.

01 · LUNA-X Adaptive Cognitive Helmet

A Second Set of Eyes on Every Mission

When an astronaut is tired, stressed or in trouble, they are often the last to notice. The helmet watches their eyes, breathing and surroundings, warns them gently before small problems become big ones, and calls the crew automatically if something goes wrong.

02 · LUNA-X Adaptive Wellness Glove

Precision When Every Movement Matters

Designed to help astronauts work with precision while protecting their hands during long lunar missions. An AI-integrated glove for health monitoring and interaction. It watches the hand itself (tremor, grip force, fatigue), scans plants and materials, talks through a 6-state light protocol and haptics, and takes gesture commands. The sensor liner is worn alone inside the habitat and inside the pressure glove on moonwalks.

03 · LUNA-X Adaptive Mobility Boot

Built for Every Step Beyond Earth

Every step our astronauts take on the Moon depends on footwear that protects them from sharp regolith, extreme cold and the risk of a fall far from help. An AI-assisted mobility platform. A smart-sole sensor array reads gait, balance and fatigue, the shock-absorber system handles impacts, an electrodynamic dust shield protects the boot, and navigation lighting guides the crew home.

Made by Students, for the Explorers of Tomorrow

LUNA-X was designed, modeled and animated by Jaineil R. for NASA HUNCH Project ARES, together with teammates Angel C. and Farhan K., who built the Lunar Smart Adaptive Habitat that LUNA-X connects to. We started with one question: how do you take care of someone when home is 384,000 km away?
