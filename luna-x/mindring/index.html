MindRing
Monitoring mental health beyond Earth.

A mission wellness device for long-duration lunar crews. MindRing reads the body's quiet stress signals day and night, keeps the raw data on the ring, asks before it assumes, and lets the habitat answer: warmer light, a calmer soundscape, an open greenhouse door. A human always decides.

Problem

Long-duration missions challenge more than the body

Isolation, confinement, disrupted sleep and distance from home build up slowly on a long mission. NASA's Human Research Program tracks behavioral health as one of the key human risks of exploration, and analog missions such as HERA and CHAPEA (Mars Dune Alpha) study it on Earth. Crews often under-report strain, and today's wearables are fitness devices first: they count steps and give a generic "stress" number. On the Moon, a missed early warning costs sleep, performance and safety.

CORE MISSION
  • Detect elevated stress early, against your own baseline
  • Track recovery, resilience and sleep quality over weeks
  • Build emotional self-awareness through check-ins, not surveillance
  • Personalize support and learn what actually helps
  • Stay preventative and non-diagnostic; a human always decides

Hardware design

Why this is the strongest ring design

01 · ACCURACY

Two ways to read HRV

Continuous PPG for trends, plus a titanium ECG inlay on the outside: touch it with the other hand for a 30-second, medical-grade HRV reading whenever the ring flags a change.

02 · PRIVACY

AI on the ring itself

A microcontroller with a neural processing unit runs the stress model on the ring. Raw physiology never has to leave your finger; only the scores you choose to share do.

03 · SUPPORT

It answers, quietly

A haptic motor taps out breathing rhythms and gentle nudges. A tiny teal light confirms a check-in. No screen, no notifications fighting for attention.

AI mental-health engine

Five scores, measured against you

Everyone's "normal" is different. MindRing spends its first 14 days learning your baseline (resting heart rate, HRV, temperature rhythm, sleep), then scores each day against it. Every score shows the top reasons behind it, so the AI is never a black box.

It asks. It doesn't assume.

The ring detects physiological patterns, never emotions. When something changes, it asks you to confirm. Your answer is a training label: the model gets more personal with every check-in, and a "No" is just as valuable as a "Yes". Try it:

Adaptive intervention system

Support that scales with the need

STRESS SPIKE

In the moment

A haptic breathing rhythm (4-7-8 or box breathing), a calming visualization in the app, and lighting advice (warmer, dimmer).

POOR RECOVERY

Over the day

Sleep-hygiene tips, an evening screen-time reminder, and light-exposure timing to shift a drifting body clock.

ISOLATION SIGNS

Over the week

Low activity + low check-in mood + flat routines prompt a nudge to call a friend, join a crew meal or a community activity.

Wellness escalation ladder

  1. Notice. A sustained change from your baseline. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  2. Ask. A gentle check-in on the ring or phone. You can always dismiss it.
  3. Support. Personalized techniques from CBT, mindfulness and positive psychology; the engine learns which ones work for you.
  4. Check in more. If a change lasts for weeks, check-ins become more frequent and self-care routines are suggested.
  5. Connect. Only with your prior opt-in: suggest contacting a trusted person or support resources, or (for crews) the flight surgeon. A human always decides.

MindRing is a wellness device. It does not diagnose or treat any medical or mental-health condition, and it is not a crisis service.

On the Moon, MindRing feeds the habitat's LUNA AI with consented scores only, alongside the helmet, glove and boot. The habitat answers with lighting, sound and greenhouse access, and the flight surgeon is one call away (1.3 s delay).

Data ethics and privacy

Built to earn trust

Consent first

Opt-in for each data type and each sharing target, revocable in one tap.

Local by default

Inference on the ring, personalization on the phone, cloud only by choice.

Encrypted

AES-256 at rest and over Bluetooth; backups with a key only the user holds.

No emotion surveillance

It reports body signals and asks; it never labels feelings or reports them to anyone.

You own your data

Export everything, delete everything, any time.

Transparent AI

Every score lists its top drivers and the model's confidence.

Risks · manufacturing · commercialization

From prototype to product

Risk assessment

  • Over-reliance: non-diagnostic messaging, human escalation
  • False alarms: ECG confirmation + check-in before action
  • Skin contact: ISO 10993 biocompatible materials
  • Battery: protected cell, thermal cut-off
  • Data breach: on-device design limits exposure

Manufacturing

  • CNC titanium shell, PVD finish
  • Zirconia face, laser-cut sensor windows
  • Rigid-flex PCB wrapped in the band
  • Epoxy potting for IP68
  • Prototype target: under $120 per unit at 1k

Go to market

  • Phase 1: analog-habitat and research crews
  • Phase 2: university wellness programs
  • Phase 3: consumer ring, optional subscription for advanced insights
  • Pursue FDA wellness-device guidance; no diagnostic claims
