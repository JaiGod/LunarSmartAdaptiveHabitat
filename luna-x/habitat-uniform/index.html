01 · The uniform

Made for the Inside of a Moon Base

This isn't a spacesuit. It's what a crew member wears every day inside the habitat: a soft, breathable one-piece flight suit for science, maintenance and long shifts. No hard armor, no helmet. It's built to be lived in.

02 · LUNA-X chest module

Life Support System Online

The circular module from the LUNA-X suit sits on a soft heart-monitor strap. Its ring turns slowly, a pulse goes out every few seconds, and the edge lights cycle while it watches the crew member's heart, breathing and temperature.

03 · Systems

Four Jobs, One Small Device

Life support, communications, biometric tracking and thermal control all report to the chest module. Wires run in fabric keepers to the wrist tablet and the radio, so nothing snags.

04 · Try it

Tap the Chest Module

Click the glowing ring to run a crew scan. A sweep passes over the uniform and the live readings appear around the module.

Product description

Lunar Habitat Operations Uniform

Designed for astronauts working inside long-duration lunar habitats, the Lunar Habitat Operations Uniform provides comfort, durability, and mobility during daily scientific, engineering, and maintenance activities. Lightweight technical fabrics support extended wear, while integrated communication and monitoring systems keep crew members connected throughout the habitat.

What's on it

Every Piece Has a Job

A real crew uniform reads as "astronaut" because of the small things: the patches, the stitching, the pockets that have clearly been used. Here's what's on this one.

Top

  • NASA-blue woven twill, collared, zip front
  • NASA HUNCH · Project ARES patch on the chest
  • "Commander" name tag and US flag
  • NASA HUNCH logo and LHC-1 mission patch on the shoulders
  • Double-needle stitching and felled seams
  • Zip chest pockets, natural fabric folds

Pants

  • Bellows cargo pockets with snap flaps
  • Reinforced ripstop knee and hip panels
  • Utility loops and zip hip pockets
  • Work belt with quick-release buckle and tool attachments
  • Hem stacks naturally over the boots

Boots

  • Lightweight indoor habitat safety boots
  • Anti-slip lugged rubber sole with grey midsole
  • Two hook-and-loop straps, no laces to snag
  • Protective toe and heel caps, textured leather

Crew gear

  • LUNA-X circular chest module on a heart-monitor strap
  • Tablet clipped to the belt, wrist tablet, smartwatch
  • Radio comms unit with earpiece and coiled cable
  • Habitat access badge on a reel
  • Emergency O2 mask pouch with quick-connect, flashlight, multitool
