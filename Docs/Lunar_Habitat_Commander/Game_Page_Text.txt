TITLE
Lunar Habitat Commander

SHORT LINE (tagline)
Keep a Moon crew healthy and happy for 30 days.

KIND OF PROJECT
Downloadable game for Windows. Classification: Games. Genre: Simulation / Educational.

DESCRIPTION
You are the commander of a smart habitat on the Moon. Four astronauts live and work inside, and the
habitat's AI, ARIA, watches their stress, sleep and mood.

Walk through the habitat - airlock, hygiene, crew quarters, galley, workstations and AI hub, storage,
life support, medical bay, greenhouse and a quiet sanctuary - and answer alerts before they turn into
emergencies: a solar storm, a broken CO2 scrubber, a pressure leak.

Between alerts, look after the crew. Share a meal, grow plants, call home, look at the Earth.
A happy crew makes fewer mistakes.

Features
- Three ways to play: Survival mission, Creative mode, and Autopilot (the AI plays; press Space or A to take over).
- Thirteen extra maps: Solar farm, Moon mining pit, Alien zap range, Play park, Beat Asteroid (a rhythm game),
  Tower defense, Factory Land (mine, smelt, build and garden), Star Patrol (fly a star fighter), Story Studio
  (paint, a story game, alien stories, light swords), Star Clicker (an idle clicker about a star's life),
  Science Academy (force diagrams from Physics 1, 2 and C, biology in space, a Hall of Science), Clinic and
  Pet House (care for patients and raise Lumi the moon pup) and a Boss Arena. Nobody is ever hurt.
  Up to four players take turns and keep their own scores, levels, costumes and pet.
- Your astronaut levels up: a green-screen level card, a present to open, star coins, a shop and costumes,
  with seasonal decorations and limited costumes that follow the real calendar.
- A crew radio: talk into your microphone and the crew answers. Only loudness is measured; nothing is recorded.
- A Crew Health System for the four crew (vitals, pulse waves, AI response), security cameras, team meetings
  with a vote, a Battery Bank to fill, astronaut food and tablets to pick up and read, including a
  Physics and Calculus reference sheet with live numbers from the game.
- Music choices: space ambient, happy EDM, lo-fi, chiptune, or your own music folder.
- A story introduction, a simple screen that shows only what matters, and "YOU DID IT!" accomplishments.
- Repair mini-games: join the wires, lock the vent on the green bar, press every health-monitor button,
  flip the light switches, repeat the computer's colour code.
- Health and hunger in Survival: eat food packs, dodge alien bumps, call the food, medic or repair rover.
- First-person and third-person view, and an astronaut dance.
- A relay satellite linked to Earth, orbital cannons that shoot down meteorites, dust storms and solar flares.
- A Dyson swarm: a few collector panels round the Sun that beam power to a dish on the Moon.
- Ten of my own 3D models: spaceships and a jet circling the base, two UFOs, a satellite, a rocket on its pad,
  a Moon outpost, a telescope and Mars rovers you can ride.
- Play with a keyboard and mouse, or in VR with a PC VR headset (tested with Meta Quest + Link).
- ARIA gives advice, forecasts problems and answers questions. Type to her, or use your voice.
- Green arrows, a minimap, a full map and a compass show the way to the next job.
- Sliding doors, a supply rocket you can launch, lunar day and night, particle effects.
- Moon Frontier: a mining field outside with water ice, ilmenite ore and helium-3. Mine it, carry it in your
  backpack and turn it into water, oxygen and power.
- Friendly-looking aliens beamed down by a UFO. Zap them and they are beamed home. Zapper, freeze ray,
  plasma launcher, mining drill and a block builder for Minecraft-style building.
- Make friends with aliens, ride the rovers, throw moon rocks, press the light and fan buttons in every zone.
- Upgrades (including a guard turret), a glowing power-cable network, ventilation fans, shooting stars.
- Kid mode: more time on every alert, gentler rules, nobody gets hurt.

This is a student project made for NASA HUNCH: "Lunar Smart Adaptive Habitat Design with AI
Psychological Monitoring". It is not an official NASA product.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Download the zip and unzip it.
2. Open the folder "Lunar Habitat Commander" and run Lunar_Habitat_Commander.exe.
3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway.
   (This appears for any new game that is not signed by a big company.)

CONTROLS
Keyboard and mouse: W A S D walk, mouse look, E act, T talk, N map, L launch rocket, Esc menu, H help.
                    R change tool, click use tool, I backpack, O lights, J go to the aliens, Shift run,
                    Space jump, F fly, X eat, V first / third person, B dance, U simple / full screen.
VR (one job per button): left stick walk (click = run), right stick turn, right grip teleport
    (hold to aim, let go), right trigger select / use, A jump, B next tool, X go to the next job, Y menu.
    Point the laser at an orb, a menu button or a panel and pull the trigger.

TWO WAYS TO PLAY
NASA HUNCH mode ENABLED (how the game opens): only the Moon base, the crew and the project. A regolith radiation
cover over the crew habitat, greenhouses outside in sunlight, an aquaponics fish tank inside, sealed food on
pantry racks, a space toilet and wash station, the AI crew-health system with consent, and a checklist that ticks
itself for every item the project sheet asks for. No aliens, portals or mini-games.
NASA HUNCH mode DISABLED: everything, including 13 extra maps reached through a plaza of animated portals.
Physics view (F8): force arrows, a gravity vector field, a live graph and the base's sensor network, with a
reading sheet from mechanics to linear algebra.

WHAT YOU NEED
Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). A graphics card is recommended. About 1 GB of free space.
VR is optional: a PC VR headset through OpenXR (Meta Quest with Link, or SteamVR).
It does not run on a Quest by itself, on a Mac or on a phone.
Voices use the voices installed in Windows. Speech-to-text needs Windows online speech recognition.
