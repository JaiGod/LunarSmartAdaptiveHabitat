TITLE
Lunar Smart Adaptive Habitat

SHORT DESCRIPTION (one line)
A 4-person lunar habitat where an AI watches crew mental health and quietly adapts light, sound, scent and air — NASA HUNCH Project ARES.

DESCRIPTION
Walk a complete lunar base in 1/6 g: four private sleep domes, a wardroom, lab, greenhouse, exercise bay, medical/storm shelter, airlock with suitports and a rover.
An AI psychological-monitoring system reads 9 sensors (never a camera or mic in private rooms), scores six conditions — anxiety, depression, conflict, fatigue, autonomy deprivation, sensory monotony — and responds in three tiers:
• Tier 1: silent, reversible environment changes (always in your personal log)
• Tier 2: a gentle offer you can accept or decline
• Tier 3: a human takes over (CMO → Commander → Mission Control), and you are told first
You are the Commander. Sleep in your pod, exercise, go on EVA, run the team meeting — or play the Agency Review Board and try to break the AI: fail a sensor, refuse an offer, hit the AI off switch.

HOW TO PLAY
WASD move · mouse look · E use · Tab crew interface · M annotated floor plan · F1 AI dashboard · F2 CMO console · F5 board scenarios Q1–Q6 · F4 72-hour tabletop · F11 Project ARES deliverables · O AI off switch · H help
