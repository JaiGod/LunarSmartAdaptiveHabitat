About the film

The story

Four astronauts land at the lunar south pole and wake a habitat that robots built for them. Their AI, LUNA, watches the air, water, power and crew, explains what it is doing, and lets people make the decisions. They survive a pump failure and a solar storm, grow their first meal on the Moon, build a city, and a generation later a child born there looks back at Earth.

How it was made

  • The whole habitat, suits and sets modeled and animated in Blender, rendered in EEVEE: about 63,000 frames.
  • 209 lines of dialogue. Each one sounds like where it's heard: suit radio, the Earth link with its 2.6-second delay, voice commands, habitat speakers.
  • The companion app, crew monitor and LUNA screens from this project appear inside the film.
