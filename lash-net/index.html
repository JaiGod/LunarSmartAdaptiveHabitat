What Luna is allowed to do

Four tiers, with a hard floor under all of them: Luna may cut anything except the link that keeps the crew breathing. Only a human can take life support offline.

LOW

Log and notify. The access list already dropped the packet. Luna records it and the duty crew member sees it on the next watch handover.

MEDIUM

Isolate the device. The switch port moves into the quarantine VLAN. The device keeps its link so Luna can keep watching it, but it can no longer reach anything.

HIGH

Quarantine the subnet and block the source. A deny rule goes in at the top of the edge access list and the affected VLAN gateway is shut.

CRITICAL

Lock down. The uplink to Earth is cut and compromised systems are disconnected. Habitat, medical and data-centre traffic keep flowing inside the base.

Limits Luna cannot cross

These are written into the design, not left to the AI to decide.

Shut down life supportnever
Cut medical monitoringnever
Lock a crew member out of commsnever
Act without writing a log linenever
Isolate a crew deviceyes, reversible
Cut the Earth uplinkyes, reversible

Every action Luna takes appears in the console log at the bottom of this page with a timestamp, and every one of them can be undone by a crew member with the console password.

What this page is. A simulation written in plain JavaScript, running entirely in your browser. There is no live network behind it and no real AI model — the detection logic is a set of rules I wrote, matching the access lists in the build guide. The point is to show the design working; the configurations that make it real are on the build guide page.
